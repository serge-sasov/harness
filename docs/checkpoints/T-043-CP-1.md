## Scope

Chunk-level verification: a new check type in `verify-artifacts.ps1`, the three
check ids in the stage registries, and their documentation.

## Artifacts

- `harness/scripts/verify-artifacts.ps1`: new type `chunk-ledger` with three
  rules (`ownership`, `coverage`, `budget`).
  - `ownership` delegates to `chunk-validate.py --json --no-hash` and fails on
    schema, duplicate id, parent/depth, state, role area, `owns` overlap,
    produced-outside-owns, missing produced file, and hash mismatch.
  - `coverage` fails on `E_COVERAGE` and `E_OWN_OUTSIDE_CARD`.
  - `budget` reads the ledger budget and fails when one produced file exceeds
    `max_write_kb` or one chunk's produced total exceeds `max_session_kb`.
  - A missing ledger is `NOT_APPLICABLE` with the reason `legacy task`.
- `harness/checks/feature.json`: the three checks appended to A-01, A-02, U-01,
  U-02, R-01, R-02, I-01, I-02, Q-01, Q-02, D-01.
- `harness/checks/bugfix.json`: the three checks appended to B-00..B-04.
- `harness/process.md` 11.1: the check ids and the `NOT_APPLICABLE` rule.
- `harness/agents/harness-coder.md`, `harness-architect.md`: the check ids in the
  verification sections.
