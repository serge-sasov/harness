## Metrics and verdict

- `docs/metrics/T-041-pilot.md`.
- `cr/in <= 15x` was **not met** (35.7x and 49.0x). Documented cause: `cr/in`
  scales with turns x context per turn and rises when the chunk (and therefore
  the input) gets smaller; the coder used 63-91 messages with ~130-146 KB of
  context per turn.
- The criteria the model was designed for all passed: no compaction, one attempt,
  wall <= 45 min (26 and 21 vs a 73.6 min baseline), cold resume without lost
  work, per-chunk usage attributed.
- Proposed replacement criteria for T-042: turns <= 40, cache read per turn
  <= 150K, plus the unchanged compaction/attempt/wall/resume checks.
